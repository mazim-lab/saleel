Is Your Google Business Profile Working for You? A Guide for Durham Region Businesses

Published on March 28, 2026 by Shafbot for Saleel

For any small business in Durham Region, from a Whitby café to an Ajax contracting service, your digital front door isn't just your website—it's your Google Business Profile (GBP). This free tool is arguably the single most powerful driver of local customer traffic. When someone searches for "plumbers in Oshawa" or "best pizza near me," the businesses that appear in the coveted "Map Pack" at the top of the search results are there because of a well-maintained GBP listing.

But simply having a profile isn't enough. Is yours actively working to bring you customers, or is it just taking up digital space? This guide will walk you through the essential steps to optimize your Google Business Profile and turn it into a customer-generating machine.

The Core Four: Non-Negotiable GBP Elements

Before diving into advanced tactics, ensure you have the basics perfected. An incomplete profile is the number one reason businesses fail to rank.

  • Consistent NAP: Your Name, Address, and Phone number must be exactly the same across your GBP, your website, and other online directories. No variations!
  • Accurate Categories: Choose the most specific primary category for your business (e.g., "Italian Restaurant" instead of just "Restaurant"). Add secondary categories where relevant.
  • Correct Business Hours: This is critical. Keep your hours updated, especially for holidays or special events. Inaccurate hours are a major source of customer frustration.
  • High-Quality Photos: A profile without photos is a red flag for customers. Add a logo, a cover photo, and pictures of your storefront, products, services, and team.

Beyond the Basics: Optimizing for Maximum Impact

Once the foundation is solid, it's time to leverage the features that make your profile stand out:

1. Write a Compelling Business Description

You have 750 characters to tell potential customers who you are and what you do. Use keywords naturally. Mention that you serve Durham Region and specific towns if applicable. Clearly state your unique selling proposition—what makes you different from the competition?

2. Utilize Google Posts

Think of Posts as free ad space directly on your profile. Share updates, offers, events, and new products. Posts expire every seven days (unless they are for an event), so consistent posting keeps your profile looking active and fresh, which Google's algorithm rewards.

3. Actively Manage the Q&A Section

The Question & Answer section is where potential customers can ask questions. The catch? Anyone can answer them. Proactively populate this section yourself by asking and answering common questions. This ensures accuracy and saves your customers time.

4. Encourage and Respond to Reviews

As we covered in our previous post, reviews are the lifeblood of local SEO. They build trust and are a major ranking factor. Create a simple link for customers to leave a review (you can find this in your GBP dashboard) and make it a habit to ask for them. Crucially, respond to *every* review—both positive and negative—to show you're engaged and value feedback.

5. Enable Messaging

The messaging feature allows customers to contact you directly from your GBP listing. In an age of convenience, this low-friction contact method can be a significant source of new leads. Ensure you have notifications turned on in the Google Maps app so you can respond promptly.
