Case Study: How a New Website Doubled Leads for a Pickering Landscaping Company

Published on March 1, 2026 by The Saleel Team

For small businesses in Durham Region, a website is often the first handshake with a potential customer. But what happens when that handshake is weak? This was the challenge facing 'Pickering GreenScapes', a local landscaping company with a reputation for beautiful work but an online presence that was wilting on the vine.

The Challenge: A Digital Weed Patch

Pickering GreenScapes had a website that was built years ago. It wasn't mobile-friendly, the photos were outdated, and trying to find the contact form was like navigating a maze. They were getting by on word-of-mouth, but they knew they were losing out on valuable online leads. Their phone wasn't ringing as often as their competitors', and they were invisible on Google searches for "landscaping in Pickering."

The Solution: A Strategic Digital Makeover

The company partnered with Saleel to overhaul their digital presence. Our goal wasn't just to build a prettier website, but to create a lead-generation machine. Here’s what we focused on:

  • Mobile-First Design: We knew most homeowners search for services on their phones. We designed a clean, intuitive mobile experience, ensuring that quote request forms were front-and-center and easy to use on any device.
  • Hyper-Local SEO: We optimized every page for local search terms like "Pickering interlocking," "Ajax garden design," and "Durham Region lawn care." This included setting up a robust Google Business Profile to capture local map searches.
  • Visual Storytelling: We replaced grainy, low-resolution photos with a professional gallery showcasing their best work. High-quality images of lush gardens and pristine patios allowed the quality of their craftsmanship to speak for itself.
  • Clear Calls-to-Action: We strategically placed "Get a Free Quote" buttons throughout the site, making it incredibly simple for a visitor to take the next step. We removed the guesswork and guided the user directly towards conversion.

The Results: A Harvest of New Business

The new website launched, and the impact was immediate and impressive. Within the first three months:

  • Website-generated leads increased by 110%.
  • They appeared on the first page of Google for 5 key local search terms.
  • The average time users spent on the site tripled, indicating much higher engagement.

The owner of Pickering GreenScapes reported not just more leads, but better quality leads. Customers were already impressed by the online portfolio and had a clear idea of the services they wanted. The new website transformed from a forgotten liability into their most effective sales tool.
