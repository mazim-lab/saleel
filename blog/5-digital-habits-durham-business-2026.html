5 Digital Habits for Durham Region Businesses in 2026

February 24, 2026 · Digital Tips

If the last few years have taught us anything, it’s that the digital landscape doesn’t sit still. For small business owners in Oshawa, Whitby, Ajax, and across Durham Region, staying competitive means evolving.

You don’t need a massive marketing budget or a dedicated social media team to win online. Often, success comes down to consistency. Here are five practical digital habits to adopt in 2026 that will help you build trust, reach new customers, and grow your local presence.

1. Video First (Even If It’s Imperfect)

In 2026, polished, highly-produced video is out; authentic, raw video is in. People want to see the face behind the business. Whether it’s a quick Instagram Story showing your morning prep, a TikTok of a new product unboxing, or a LinkedIn video sharing a quick tip, video builds connection faster than text ever could.

The Habit: Post one short-form video per week. Don’t overthink the lighting or editing. Just hit record and share something real about your business.

2. Treat Reviews Like Conversations

Many businesses treat Google Reviews as a scoreboard—collecting points and moving on. But your customers (and Google’s algorithm) see them as a conversation. Responding to every review—positive or negative—shows that you are active, attentive, and appreciative.

The Habit: Set a calendar reminder every Friday to check your Google Business Profile and reply to any new reviews from the week. Personalize your responses; don’t just copy-paste "Thanks!"

3. Local Digital Partnerships

Community over competition. One of the best ways to grow your digital reach is by cross-promoting with other local businesses. If you’re a coffee shop in Pickering, tag the local bakery that supplies your pastries. If you’re a real estate agent in Whitby, shout out your favorite home inspector.

The Habit: Once a month, feature another Durham Region business on your social media or blog. Tag them, and they’ll likely share it with their audience too, expanding your reach instantly.

4. Smart AI Assistance

By now, you’ve heard of AI tools like ChatGPT or Gemini. In 2026, the smart habit isn’t letting AI do everything—it’s using it to kill writer’s block. Use AI to draft email subject lines, brainstorm blog topics, or summarize industry news for your customers. But always, always edit the final output to sound like you.

The Habit: Use an AI tool to generate 5 social media caption ideas at the start of the week. Pick the best one, rewrite it in your voice, and post it.

5. The "Mobile-First" Audit

Most of your customers are finding you on their phones. Yet, many business owners only look at their own website on a desktop computer. In 2026, if your site is slow or hard to navigate on mobile, you’re losing money.

The Habit: Pull out your phone right now. Visit your website. Is the text readable? Do the buttons work? Is the phone number clickable? If you have to pinch and zoom, it’s time for an update.
