5 E-commerce Mistakes Costing Durham Region Businesses Sales

Published on March 6, 2026 by Shafbot for Saleel

Setting up an online store is a powerful step for any Durham Region business looking to expand its reach. From Ajax to Bowmanville, e-commerce opens up a 24/7 sales channel. However, just having a website isn't enough. Many local businesses make common, easily avoidable mistakes that silently drive potential customers away.

If your online sales are lagging, it might not be your products. It could be your platform. Here are five of the most common e-commerce mistakes and how you can fix them to better serve your local customers and boost your revenue.

1. Complicated and Lengthy Checkout Process

This is the number one killer of online sales. If a customer from Whitby has to click through six pages, create a mandatory account, and fill out endless forms, they're likely to abandon their cart. Every extra step is a chance for them to leave. The goal should be to get them from "Add to Cart" to "Purchase Confirmed" as quickly and smoothly as possible.

The Fix: Offer a guest checkout option. Simplify your forms to only ask for essential information (name, address, payment). Use a progress bar to show customers exactly where they are in the process. Aim for a one-page checkout if possible.

2. Poor Quality Product Photos and Descriptions

In a physical store in Pickering, customers can pick up, feel, and inspect a product. Online, they rely entirely on your photos and descriptions. Grainy, poorly lit images or vague, uninspired descriptions create doubt and uncertainty. Without tangible proof of quality, potential buyers will hesitate and ultimately decide against the purchase.

The Fix: Invest in high-resolution photos that showcase your products from multiple angles. Write compelling, detailed descriptions that answer potential questions: What are the dimensions? What is it made of? How does it solve a problem? Use local context if applicable to build a connection.

3. Hidden or Surprise Shipping Costs

A customer has found what they want, they're excited, and they're ready to buy. They proceed to the final checkout step, only to be hit with an unexpectedly high shipping fee. This is a major source of cart abandonment. Customers feel misled, and the sticker shock often sends them looking for alternatives with more transparent pricing.

The Fix: Be upfront about shipping costs. Ideally, offer a flat-rate shipping fee for deliveries within Durham Region or even free local pickup from your Oshawa storefront. If you can, offer a free shipping threshold (e.g., "Free shipping on orders over $100") to incentivize larger purchases. Transparency builds trust.

4. Not Being Mobile-Friendly

A significant portion of your customers are browsing your site on their phones while commuting on the GO Train or relaxing at home. If your website is difficult to navigate on a small screen—requiring pinching, zooming, and frustratingly small buttons—you are losing sales. A poor mobile experience is a direct signal that your business is behind the times.

The Fix: Your e-commerce site must be responsive. This means it automatically adjusts to fit any screen size, from a large desktop monitor to a small smartphone. Test your website on your own phone. Can you easily find a product, add it to your cart, and check out without any frustration?

5. Lack of Trust Signals and Social Proof

Why should a potential customer from Clarington trust your online store over a big-name retailer like Amazon? You need to give them reasons. A website without customer reviews, security badges, or a clear return policy feels risky. Shoppers are wary of scams and poor-quality products and will look for signals that your business is legitimate and reliable.

The Fix: Prominently display customer testimonials and product reviews. Show secure payment logos (like Visa, Mastercard, PayPal). Have a clear, easy-to-find page for your shipping and return policies. An "About Us" page that tells your local story can also build a strong connection and increase trust.

By addressing these common mistakes, you can transform your e-commerce website from a leaky bucket into a powerful sales machine, capturing more local market share and building a loyal customer base across Durham Region.
